Below is a full redesign blueprint for all main screens of the ZRF app. The goal is to move away from “stacked card UI” and toward a clean sports dashboard style that uses:

grids

split panels

horizontal scrollers

fewer rounded containers

clearer hierarchy

Think Strava + Apple Fitness + Notion dashboards.
