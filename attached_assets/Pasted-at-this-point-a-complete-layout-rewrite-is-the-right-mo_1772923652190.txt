The New Core Principle

The app should be organized around this logic:

Home   = my rugby today
Play   = all rugby activity and opportunities
GO     = check in / log activity
Club   = my club’s shared space
Profile = me, membership, XP, settings

That is the cleanest structure.

Final Bottom Navigation
Home | Play | GO | Club | Profile
